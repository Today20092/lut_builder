LUT Builder helps you make diagnostic false-color LUTs for exposure monitoring.
Choose your camera's log profile and display color space, set the exposure bands
and colors, then export a `.cube` file for a compatible camera, monitor, or editor.
It is free and open source under the [MIT license](https://github.com/Today20092/lut_builder/blob/main/LICENSE).

These are exposure guides, not creative grades. Colors label the ranges you
choose; they do not establish physical sensor clipping. Start with
[Make your first LUT](../first-lut/) for the full workflow and its limits.

## Your work stays on your device

The hosted builder processes LUTs and selected media in your browser. The optional
local Python workspace also runs on your own computer. Saved setups and palettes
stay in that browser; they do not sync between devices. Export setup JSON to keep
a portable backup before clearing browser storage.

## Help and contribute

Read the [manual](../) for controls, export instructions, and troubleshooting.
Visit the [GitHub project](https://github.com/Today20092/lut_builder) for source code,
releases, and contribution guidance. To report a problem or suggest an improvement,
[open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/Today20092/lut_builder/issues/new/choose).
Include your camera log/gamut, the device applying the LUT, and steps to reproduce
the problem. Remove private information from attachments first.
